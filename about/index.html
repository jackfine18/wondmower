About Us

Built for the Ground Others Avoid

WOND Mower is a specialized manufacturer of remote-control mowing machinery. We focus on one thing: letting operators cut grass, brush and vegetation on dangerous or hard-to-reach terrain without ever stepping onto it.

Our machines serve a wide range of users — orchard managers, solar farm operators, municipal greening teams, and contractors maintaining river embankments and highway slopes.

What Sets Us Apart

  • Safety by design — full remote control keeps people away from slopes, heat and rotating blades.
  • Built to last — reinforced steel frames and industrial rubber tracks for daily commercial use.
  • Flexible manufacturing — OEM / ODM customization of mowing width, engine, tracks and branding.
  • Export ready — wooden-crate packaging and worldwide shipping with full documentation.

Our Facility

Based in Jining, Shandong — a major machinery manufacturing hub in China — our workshop handles welding, assembly, testing and export packing under one roof.
