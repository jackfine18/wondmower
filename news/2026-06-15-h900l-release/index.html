New H-H900L Climbs Slopes Up to 60°

Jun 15, 2026

We are pleased to launch the H-H900L, our steep-slope specialist. With a reinforced track drive it climbs embankments and terraced orchards up to 60°, keeping operators safely 300m away.

The 900mm cutting deck clears brush and grass in a single pass, making it a strong fit for highway slopes, reservoir banks and hillside vineyards. The P variant adds extra underbody protection for rocky ground.

Available now for distributors and direct industrial buyers, with OEM/ODM options.
