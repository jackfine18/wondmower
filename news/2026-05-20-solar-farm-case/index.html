Case Study: Solar Farm Vegetation Control

May 20, 2026

A 20MW solar plant in northern China replaced manual brush cutting with our H-800L / H-1000L remote-control mowers.

Results:

  • Vegetation kept consistently low under panels, reducing shading loss.
  • Eliminated the need for workers to walk between panel rows — lower injury and fire risk.
  • Maintenance labor dropped by roughly 60% versus manual cutting.

Remote operation lets a single operator manage large panel arrays from the perimeter, making it ideal for solar, wind and reclamation sites.
