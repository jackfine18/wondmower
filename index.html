Who We Are

A specialized manufacturer of remote-control mowing machinery for industrial and agricultural landscaping.

WOND Mower is dedicated to building robust, remote-controlled mowing machines that take people out of harm's way. From steep river embankments to dense solar farms and orchard rows, our tracked and wheeled platforms deliver consistent cutting performance where traditional ride-on mowers cannot operate safely.

Every machine is engineered with a reinforced steel frame, reliable remote telemetry and export-grade wooden-crate packaging — backed by OEM/ODM flexibility for distributors worldwide.
